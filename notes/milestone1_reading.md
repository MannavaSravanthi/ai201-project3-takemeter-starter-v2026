# Milestone 1 — Community and reading notes

**Community:** r/Arkansas, posts with the **News** flair (public subreddit).

**What I read:** ~28 recent News-flaired posts (Oct 13–17, 2026). Nearly all are a headline plus a link to a local news outlet, with little or no body text.

**Why not just use the subreddit's flair as labels:** flair (News, Discussion, Politics…) is chosen by the poster and describes the *kind of submission*, not the subject. Every post I read was "News", so the flair can't distinguish them.

## Candidate distinctions I saw in the text

1. **Crime / legal stories:** a named person arrested, charged, or sentenced, or a body found; also lawsuits and court rulings ("Arkansas one of 8 states suing OpenAI").
2. **Elections and politicians:** candidates, campaigns, debates, voting logistics, politicians' visits or accusations ("Free rides to polls available for Washington County voters").
3. **Institutional / community announcements:** school closures, new facilities, awards, surveys, health and weather warnings ("Mountain Valley Spring Water opens new manufacturing facility").
4. **Opinion pieces:** rare. Only one clear one ("Why I don't feel sorry for Arkansas farmers"). Later dropped as a label because there are too few (see README).

## Things to watch for when collecting
- Same story posted twice (e.g. the two Little Rock school-closure posts, the two Carney sentencings) → near-duplicates can leak across the train/test split. Keep one.
- Some stories are about Arkansans outside Arkansas (Lake Mead, Saudi airport attack). Still in scope if posted under News.
